# Poulomi Florique Design Map

## Research Notes

- Official Poulomi project page positions Florique as an ongoing residential project in Thanisandra, Bengaluru with RERA PRM/KA/RERA/1251/446/PR/180326/008539, 3 BHK focus, 720 apartments and the line "A World that Blossoms Around You."
- Official Florique website builds the brand around sunlight, vivid landscapes, 8.66 acres, 85% open spaces, bloomscapes, water security, IGBC Platinum pre-certification and North Bengaluru convenience.
- Official contact page confirms Poulomi Florique LLP, SY No. 16 Kannur Village, Bidara Halli Hobli, Bengaluru Urban, Karnataka 560077, phone +91 91192 39119 and floriquesales@poulomi.in.
- Independent real-estate pages use fast decision patterns: hero facts, price from, floor plan tables, master plan proof, amenity icons, location advantages, brochure/cost-sheet CTAs and persistent mobile enquiry.
- Competitor pages often feel listing-heavy and transactional. The opportunity is to keep their information clarity but present it with a calmer, more premium editorial experience.

## Design Direction

- Mobile first: every section starts as a single-column journey with thumb-friendly CTAs, large imagery, compact facts and no desktop-only dependencies.
- Luxury feel: use deep Florique green, warm ivory, oxidized gold, restrained red CTA accents, tactile borders, large but controlled display typography and spacious image-led sections.
- Brand narrative: start with "A World that Blossoms Around You", then explain the idea through the four buyer signals: open spaces, larger residences, clubhouse/sports life and water-secure landscape planning.
- Conversion journey: hero enquiry and brochure CTAs, a quick proof strip, residence selector, amenity/bloomscape highlights, master plan/location proof, FAQ and final site-visit form.
- Visual system: immersive hero, editorial split sections on desktop, carousel-like horizontal cards on mobile, repeated botanical line motifs, and actual project imagery throughout.

## Page Architecture

1. Sticky premium header with brand, short nav and enquiry CTA.
2. Full-bleed mobile-first hero with official courtyard image, compact status/RERA chip and immediate enquiry form.
3. Proof strip for 8.66 acres, 85% open spaces, 720 apartments and 37,800 sq ft clubhouse.
4. Brand narrative section: "Designed for life in bloom" with official tower/clubhouse imagery and key specifications.
5. Residence selector with floor-plan image, sizes and price-on-request/price-from CTAs.
6. Bloomscapes and amenities section with botanical and sports/wellness groupings.
7. Master plan section with tappable image and key layout ideas.
8. Location proof grouped by education, healthcare, lifestyle, tech parks and transit.
9. FAQ plus final enquiry form.
10. Footer with RERA, disclaimer and contact information.
